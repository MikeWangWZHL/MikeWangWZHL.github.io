01 What we have built (Research Achievements)Three bridges toward a unified multimodal agent

Modality bridge

Make language, vision, video, structure, and action mutually legible.

  • VidIL ↗NeurIPS 2022100+ citations

    A language-first video learner that converts frames into structured image-and-language descriptors for few-shot video tasks.

  • Paxion ↗NeurIPS 2023 Spotlight50+ citations

    Diagnosed the action-knowledge gap in video-language models and patched temporal action dynamics into frozen foundation models.

  • VDLM ↗TMLRCVPR MAR Spotlight

    Introduced Primal Visual Description, a symbolic bridge from precise vector perception to language reasoning.

  • DyMU ↗NeurIPS 2025

    Dynamically merges—and virtually restores—visual tokens to accelerate VLM inference without permanently discarding information.

Reasoning bridge

Make reasoning collaborative, grounded, and perception-aware.

  • SPP ↗NAACL 2024 Main500+ citations

    An early multi-persona reasoning framework that turns one LLM into a team of specialized, self-collaborating reasoners.

  • PAPO ↗ICLR 202650+ citations

    Introduced perception-aware policy optimization, reducing multimodal perception errors while learning to reason.

  • CAMCUE ↗2026 Preprint

    Grounds natural-language perspective descriptions into camera poses and imagined target views for multi-view spatial reasoning.

  • CreativityBench ↗2026 Preprint

    Introduces a large affordance knowledge base and 14K tasks to test grounded creative tool repurposing in agents.

  • MM-CreativityBench ↗2026 Preprint

    Advances creative physical intelligence through visually grounded exploration and affordance-grounded alignment.

Action bridge

Turn a multimodal model from a brain into an adaptive actor.

  • Mobile-Agent-E ↗NeurIPS SEA Oral100+ citations

    Built a hierarchical mobile agent that improves through persistent tips, shortcuts, and past interaction experience.

  • OSExpert ↗2026 Preprint

    Enables computer-use agents to discover, verify, and compose professional GUI skills through environment exploration.

  • TriMPI ↗ICLR 2026

    Internalizes multimodal policies into conversational agents so interaction skills become native model behavior.

  • MultiAgentBench ↗ACL 2025 Main200+ citations

    A comprehensive benchmark for collaboration and competition across diverse, interactive multi-agent scenarios.

Impact first

Ownership and visibility
